Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 28, 2026

Welcome to Plagiarism Checker Plus ("we," "our," or "us"), owned and operated by Oaknova Systems LTD. We are committed to protecting your privacy while delivering high-performance plagiarism detection, text comparison, and AI paraphrasing utilities. This policy explains how we collect, handle, process, and purge your data.

1. Information We Collect

We process minimal data required to deliver our core features securely:

  • Anonymous Authentication & Account Data: Upon launching the application, an anonymous user identifier (UID) is created via Firebase Auth to maintain your credit balance and task history securely without requiring personal registration.
  • Text & Document Inputs: When you submit text or upload documents (.docx, .pdf) for plagiarism checks, comparisons, or paraphrasing, the text is processed via secure API streams strictly to deliver your analysis.
Ephemeral Processing & 30-Minute Auto-Purge

We believe your content belongs solely to you. All uploaded document files are immediately purged from our processing disks upon completion. Furthermore, background paraphrasing jobs are subject to an automatic 30-minute Time-To-Live (TTL) deletion policy in our database unless you explicitly keep them saved inside your app history.

2. Subscriptions & Payments

In-app credit purchases and subscriptions are handled through the official Apple App Store, Google Play Store, and RevenueCat.

  • We never collect, store, or view your credit card numbers or financial information.
  • All purchase receipts are validated anonymously using RevenueCat webhooks to issue credit top-ups directly to your Firebase UID.

3. Third-Party Services

To deliver our utilities, we utilize trusted infrastructure providers:

  • Firebase (Google): To manage anonymous user authentication and maintain encrypted database records.
  • Cloudflare (R2 Storage): For ephemeral, temporary encrypted file handling during document parsing.
  • RevenueCat: To process in-app credit purchases and subscription status.
  • Analysis Engines (Winston AI & CleverHumanizer): Processors used strictly to perform plagiarism verification, text comparisons, and paraphrasing.

4. Data Retention & Control

You have full control over your analysis history. You can view or delete individual history logs directly inside the app workspace at any time. If you wish to wipe your entire database history and account state, you may do so in the app Settings tab by selecting "Delete Account & Data" or by reaching out to support.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 22, 2026

Welcome to ArbiGuru ("we," "our," or "us"), owned and operated by Oaknova Systems LTD. We are committed to protecting your privacy while delivering a real-time crypto spatial arbitrage terminal. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimal data required to deliver our core features securely:

  • Account & Authentication Data: When using our app, a user identifier is managed through Firebase Auth to maintain your subscription entitlements, custom capital calculator settings, and token watchlists securely.
  • Terminal & Sizing Preferences: User preferences, such as selected simulated trading capital (e.g., $1,000 or $10,000), filter choices, and pinned pairs are saved to ensure your settings persist across sessions.
  • Market & Price Feed Information: Real-time order book spreads, prices, and transfer network statuses are retrieved from our CEX Arbitrage API backend (at api.oaknovasystems.com) strictly to display live spatial arbitrage opportunities.
  • Diagnostic Data: Basic device information and anonymized crash analytics may be collected through standard SDKs to maintain WebSocket feed stability and app performance.
Non-Custodial & Read-Only Intelligence

ArbiGuru is strictly an informational and analytical tool. We never request, collect, or store your exchange API secret keys, crypto wallet private keys, seed phrases, or funds. All data displayed is public market information.

2. Subscriptions & Payments

All in-app purchases, weekly subscriptions, and monthly passes are handled through the official Apple App Store, Google Play Store, and RevenueCat.

  • We never collect, process, or store your credit card numbers, bank credentials, or financial accounts.
  • All purchase transactions are validated anonymously through RevenueCat to issue Pro feature unlocks directly to your user account.

3. Third-Party Services

To deliver our services, we utilize trusted infrastructure providers:

  • Firebase (Google): To manage secure user authentication and synchronize user preferences.
  • RevenueCat: To manage in-app subscriptions, receipts, and Pro tier access.
  • ArbiGuru Backend (Oaknova Systems): Our backend service at api.oaknovasystems.com that aggregates live order books and verifies deposit/withdrawal chain statuses.

4. Data Retention & Control

You have full control over your data. You can reset your simulated capital, clear watchlists, or request the deletion of your account directly inside the app settings at any time or by contacting our support team.

Marry

Child Safety Standards

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

This page sets out the published standards of Marry ("we," "our," or "us"), operated by Oaknova Systems LTD, against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These standards apply to everyone who uses Marry and to all content and conduct on the platform. They are publicly available worldwide and are not editable by users.

Zero tolerance. 18+ only. Report in-app or by email.

Marry prohibits any content or behaviour that sexualises, exploits, or endangers a child. We remove such content, terminate the accounts involved, and report to the appropriate authorities.

1. Our Commitment

We have a zero-tolerance policy for CSAE and CSAM. Marry is a marriage-introduction platform for consenting adults aged 18 and over. It is not a dating app for minors, and it must never be used to contact, groom, solicit, exploit, or harm a child in any way.

We are committed to:

  • Preventing minors from creating accounts or using the service.
  • Preventing, detecting, and removing CSAE/CSAM from the platform.
  • Providing clear, accessible ways for users to report child safety concerns.
  • Cooperating fully with law enforcement, child protection agencies, and regulators.
  • Complying with applicable child safety laws in the regions where Marry is available.

2. Who These Standards Apply To

These standards apply to all users, visitors, and anyone who submits content to Marry, including account holders, guests, and people acting on behalf of others. They also apply to all content on the platform, including requests, responses, introductions, consent invitations, messages, profile details, and any files or links shared through the service.

3. Prohibited Content and Conduct

The following are strictly prohibited on Marry:

  • CSAM: Any visual or written depiction of a child engaged in, or presented for the purpose of, sexual activity, or any sexualised depiction of a child, in any format, including images, video, audio, text, links, or encoded content.
  • Child sexual exploitation: Grooming, sextortion, trafficking, solicitation, coercion, or any attempt to obtain sexual contact with, or sexual material from, a minor.
  • Minors on the platform: Creating an account or posting a request or response for, or on behalf of, anyone under 18.
  • Facilitation: Sharing links, instructions, contact details, or referrals that promote or enable CSAE/CSAM.
  • Denial or glorification: Content that normalises, denies, or encourages child sexual abuse or exploitation.

Violations may result in immediate removal of content, suspension or permanent termination of the account, preservation of evidence, and reporting to the relevant authorities.

4. Age Requirements and Prevention

  • Marry is strictly for people aged 18 and over. Requests for a wife or husband are only permitted for adults.
  • We use account and content controls to detect and remove attempts to create accounts for, or post on behalf of, minors.
  • Our automated content filter blocks language associated with child marriage, underage solicitation, and trafficking before public content is published.
  • Reports are reviewed by our moderation team, and confirmed minors are removed from the platform.
  • If you believe a minor is using Marry, report it immediately using the methods in Section 5.

5. How to Report a Child Safety Concern

If you encounter content or behaviour that may involve CSAE/CSAM, or any child safety concern, report it immediately.

In the app

  • On a request, open the request and choose Report.
  • In a conversation, open the chat menu and choose Report.
  • Select Child safety concern as the reason and add any details you can. Do not download, copy, or forward suspected CSAM; simply report it.
  • You can also block the member so they cannot contact you or respond to you.

By email

  • Designated child safety contact: oaknova.admin@oaknovasystems.com
  • General contact: contact@oaknovasystems.com

Reports are confidential. We do not tell the reported member who reported them. Deliberately false reports are also reviewed and may lead to action.

6. How We Review and Respond

Child safety reports are treated as a priority. When we receive a report, we may:

  • Review the reported content, account, and associated activity.
  • Remove the content immediately and restrict or terminate the account.
  • Preserve relevant records and evidence in accordance with our retention obligations.
  • Report the matter to the relevant authorities and, where appropriate, to child protection organisations.
  • Take additional steps needed to protect users and prevent recurrence.

7. Reporting to Authorities

We report apparent CSAM and CSAE to the appropriate regional and national authorities. Where applicable, this includes:

  • The National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and other reporting bodies for platforms serving the United States, consistent with 18 U.S.C. § 2258A.
  • National and regional law enforcement and child protection agencies in the jurisdictions where the content or the affected child is located.
  • Relevant regulators under applicable online safety laws, including the UK Online Safety Act and equivalent legislation.

Where the law requires, we preserve relevant data for the period required by law to assist investigations.

8. Cooperation with Law Enforcement

We cooperate with law enforcement and child safety organisations investigating CSAE/CSAM. We respond to valid legal requests, preserve records when required, and take steps necessary to protect children and support investigations.

9. Data Retention and Evidence

We retain reports, moderation records, and related evidence only for as long as needed to handle the report, comply with legal obligations, and protect the safety of our users. Where the law requires preservation of CSAM-related evidence, we preserve it and restrict access to authorised personnel only.

For more information about what we collect and how long we keep it, see our Privacy Policy.

10. Review of These Standards

We review these standards regularly, at least annually, and update them as our practices, technology, or legal obligations change. The effective date above shows when this version took effect.

12. Compliance Statement

Marry complies with all relevant child safety laws, and we report to regional and national authorities as described above. We are committed to keeping children safe and to preventing the use of our platform for any form of child sexual abuse or exploitation.

Marry

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 11, 2026

Welcome to Marry ("we," "our," or "us"), owned and operated by Oaknova Systems LTD. Marry is a consent-first marriage introduction platform where an adult can look for a wife or a husband for themselves, or on behalf of someone they represent. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, who can see it, and the choices you have.

Marriage only. 18+. No public personal details.

Marry is not a dating app and not a matchmaking service. Public requests never include names, photos, phone numbers, or email addresses. Personal details are shared only after an intentional unlock and, for requests or referrals about someone else, only after that person consents.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what is needed to run the service safely:

  • Account & authentication data: A guest account is created automatically when you open the app, and it can later become a full account with an email and password. Authentication is handled by Firebase Auth; we never see or store your password.
  • Request details: What you choose to publish — who the request is for (yourself or someone you represent), whether you are looking for a wife or a husband, the age range, country and optional city, a description, and optional preferences (for example religion, education, or occupation).
  • Responses and introductions: The introduction you write when you respond as yourself or refer someone, and for referrals the referred person's email address (if you provide it) and their consent decision.
  • Conversations: Messages exchanged in unlocked conversations are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). We also store conversation metadata such as participants and timestamps so the feature works. We do not use your messages for advertising.
  • Safety data: Reports you submit and members you block, so we can review content and keep people safe.
  • Subscription data: Whether your account has Marry Pro, managed through RevenueCat and the app stores. We never receive your card or bank details.
  • Diagnostic data: Basic app and device diagnostics used to fix crashes and keep the app reliable.

2. How We Use Information

  • Operate the service: publish requests, deliver responses, generate consent links, and open conversations.
  • Enforce consent: keep requests or introductions about someone else hidden until that person approves.
  • Keep the community safe: moderation, reports, blocking, abuse prevention, and enforcing our rules.
  • Manage Marry Pro entitlements and respond to support requests.
  • Comply with applicable law.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. What Other People Can See

  • Public browsing (no account needed): Active requests show the age range, country and optional city, description, optional preferences, and when the request was posted. No names, photos, contact details, or account identifiers are shown.
  • Request owners: You can see the responses to your own requests. Introductions stay hidden until you unlock a response. A free account can unlock one response; Marry Pro unlocks every response.
  • Conversations: Only the participants of an unlocked conversation can read its messages.
  • Guests: Browsing works without creating an account. If you create an account later, it is linked to the same guest profile so your activity is not lost.

4. Consent and Representative Requests

Marry is built around consent, including the tradition of finding a partner for someone else (Jagam).

  • If you post a request on behalf of someone, we collect that person's email address and generate a private consent link. The request stays hidden from the public until that person reviews and approves it. Only they can approve or decline, and a declined request does not go public.
  • If you refer someone in a response, that person must approve the consent link before your introduction is shared with the request owner and before a conversation can begin.
  • Never post another person's private information without their permission.

5. Subscriptions and Payments

Marry Pro is purchased through the Apple App Store (and, where offered, Google Play) and managed with RevenueCat.

  • We never collect, process, or store your credit card numbers, bank credentials, or financial accounts.
  • RevenueCat receives an app user ID and purchase information to validate your subscription and unlock Pro features. Payment is handled entirely by the app store.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store settings.

6. Third-Party Services

We rely on trusted infrastructure providers to deliver Marry:

  • Firebase (Google): Secure authentication, Cloud Firestore conversations, push notifications, and account deletion.
  • RevenueCat: Subscription management, receipt validation, and Pro entitlement status.
  • Marry backend (Oaknova Systems): Our service at oaknovasystems.com, which operates requests, responses, consent links, reports, and moderation.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play: Payment processing for subscriptions.

7. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We keep your data while your account is active or as needed to provide the service. Requests expire after the period shown in the app and can be closed at any time.

You can delete your account directly in the app under Account → Delete account. This marks the account as deleted, closes your requests, removes your push notification tokens, and requests deletion of your Firebase login. Some limited records may be retained where required by law or to prevent fraud and abuse (for example, anonymized audit and report records). Consent links also expire.

8. Safety, Reporting, and Blocking

You can report requests or members and block members from inside the app. Blocking hides that member's requests from you and prevents them from responding to you. We review reports and may suspend or remove accounts that violate our rules or the law.

9. Children

Marry is intended only for adults who are 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that a minor has created an account, we will delete it.

10. Your Rights and Choices

  • Browse without an account.
  • Edit or close your requests, block or unblock members, and delete your account in the app.
  • Contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA. We will honor applicable requests.

11. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections and authentication is handled by Firebase. No system is completely secure, so please keep your email and password private and use a strong, unique password.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date above and, where appropriate, let you know inside the app. Continued use of Marry after an update means you accept the revised policy.

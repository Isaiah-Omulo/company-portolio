AI Fantasy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 4, 2026

Welcome to AI Fantasy ("we," "our," or "us"), owned and operated by Oaknova Systems LTD. We are committed to protecting your privacy while delivering an AI-powered Fantasy Premier League (FPL) assistant. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimal data required to deliver our core features securely:

  • Anonymous Account Data: Upon first launch, an anonymous user identifier (UID) is created via Firebase Auth to securely maintain your saved players, favorite lists, and settings without requiring any personal registration or email address.
  • Fantasy Team Data: Players you save to your "My Team" and players you mark as favorites are stored in your anonymous account so your selections persist across devices and sessions.
  • Player & Fixture Information: Player statistics, costs, predicted points, and fixture data are retrieved from our FPL AI API (FastAPI backend at api.oaknovasystems.com) strictly to power lineup optimization, captain recommendations, and predictions.
  • Diagnostic Data: Basic device and crash diagnostic information may be collected through standard analytics to help us improve app stability and performance.
No Personal Information Required

We do not require or store your name, email address, phone number, or payment details. Your identity remains anonymous — we only track a generated user identifier to keep your saved team data separate and secure.

2. Subscriptions & Payments

Premium features, credit top-ups, and subscriptions are handled through the official Apple App Store, Google Play Store, and RevenueCat.

  • We never collect, store, or view your credit card numbers or financial information.
  • All purchase receipts are validated anonymously using RevenueCat webhooks to issue feature unlocks or credit top-ups directly to your anonymous user account.

3. Third-Party Services

To deliver our services, we utilize trusted infrastructure providers:

  • Firebase (Google): To manage anonymous user authentication and store your saved team/favorites data securely.
  • RevenueCat: To process in-app purchases, subscriptions, and entitlement status.
  • FPL AI Backend (Oaknova Systems): Our FastAPI service that computes AI lineup optimizations, player predictions, and optimal team recommendations.

4. Data Retention & Control

You have full control over your data. You can view, remove, or clear your saved team and favorite players directly inside the app at any time. If you wish to wipe your entire saved account state, you may do so in the app settings or by reaching out to support.

By using AI Fantasy, you agree to our standard terms of service. For Apple iOS users, our licensing terms conform to the standard Apple End User License Agreement (EULA).

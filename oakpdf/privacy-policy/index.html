Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 15, 2026

Welcome to OakPDF: PDF Converter & Editor ("we," "our," or "us"). We are dedicated to providing a secure, reliable, and high-performance utility to merge, split, compress, protect, scan, and convert your documents. This Privacy Policy details how we handle, process, and secure your files and metadata.

1. Information We Collect & How We Process It

To deliver our secure processing and cloud utilities, we utilize the following data models:

  • Account Data: We sign you in anonymously upon your first launch to establish a secure database workspace. If you link a permanent account (via email or Google), we process your profile metadata to preserve your settings and billing details.
  • Document & Image Files: When you select a document or image to process (e.g., merging, splitting, compressing, running text recognition, or password protecting), your files are transmitted to our secure servers for active, programmatic processing.
  • Task Metadata: We store minimal task tracking metrics (such as the operation requested, processing status, and original/modified filenames) to maintain your secure, real-time file history feed.
Automatic 24-Hour File Purge

We prioritize document privacy above all. Processed files are uploaded to highly secure, unguessable URLs on Cloudflare R2 storage [6]. We strictly do not keep your files permanently. All files uploaded during processing are automatically and permanently deleted from our servers and storage systems within 24 hours using native object lifecycles [6].

2. Device and Server Storage

Our hybrid network design separates local files from transient server processing:

  • Local Device Cache: Files you pick, view, or explicitly download are stored locally inside your mobile device's private app directory (using legacy file-system structures) [1].
  • Processing VPS: Files are processed inside secure, isolated directories on our virtual private server. Once the processing finishes, these temporary local file system directories are cleared instantly from the VPS disk.
  • Firestore Sync: Task tracking data (history log entries) are synced in real-time to our secure Google Firebase Firestore database, protected by rigid per-user rules [5].

3. Subscriptions and Payments

OakPDF offers premium features and subscriptions. All payment verification is processed securely through Apple App Store, Google Play Store, and RevenueCat.

  • We do not collect, view, or store your credit card details or financial information.
  • Subscription states are monitored via securely linked, anonymous store receipts.

4. Third-Party Services

We partner with trusted, secure industry leaders to maintain operations:

  • Firebase (Google): To power authentication services and store database records [5].
  • Cloudflare (R2 Storage): To host temporary, encrypted processing outputs under absolute zero-egress models [6].
  • RevenueCat: To manage in-app subscription entitlements securely.
  • Google Play Store / Apple App Store: For billing and app distribution.

5. Data Control & Deletion

You have full control over your documents and logs. You can delete individual items from your History tab inside the app at any time [5]. If you wish to delete your entire database workspace and all associated logs, you can do so through the Settings page or by contacting us.

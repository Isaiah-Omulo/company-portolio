Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 6, 2026

Welcome to TempNum: Receive SMS & OTP ("we," "our," or "us"), owned and operated by Oaknova Systems LTD. We are committed to protecting your personal privacy while providing temporary, private phone numbers for SMS verification and temporary communications. This Privacy Policy details the minimal data we collect, how it is used, and how you maintain total control over your information.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in strict data minimization. We only collect the minimal information required to deliver virtual communication services and maintain your credit wallet:

  • Account & Authentication Identifiers: We support both anonymous guest access and registered logins managed through Firebase Authentication. We store an anonymous user identifier (Firebase UID) to track your credit wallet balance, active rentals, and verification orders. If you register using an email address, we store your email for account recovery.
  • Verification & Number Logs: When you request a temporary phone number, our system records the allocated temporary number, the designated online service (e.g., WhatsApp, Telegram), timestamps, and the incoming verification SMS code to display it in your app.
  • Credit Ledger & Transaction History: Every purchase, deduction, and automated supplier refund is recorded in our internal double-entry ledger to guarantee auditability and prevent credit loss.
  • Diagnostics & Security Telemetry: Standard non-identifying telemetry (such as device model, OS version, and app performance logs) may be collected to monitor Server-Sent Events (SSE) connections and prevent automated abuse.
Privacy-First: Zero Access to Your Personal Device SIM

TempNum protects your personal identity. We never access, collect, or upload your personal device's real phone number, your address book/contacts, personal photo library, or SMS messages stored on your physical SIM card. All operations occur strictly through our cloud-provisioned virtual numbers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for the following purposes:

  • Provisioning temporary virtual numbers and retrieving verification SMS codes from telecommunication routing suppliers.
  • Maintaining your credit balance and processing instant automated refunds if a supplier fails to allocate a number or if a verification expires.
  • Transmitting real-time SMS push events to your device using Server-Sent Events (SSE).
  • Preventing fraud, spam, automated script abuse, and platform exploitation.

3. In-App Purchases & Payments

All credit purchases are processed directly through the official Apple App Store, Google Play Store, and RevenueCat.

  • We never collect, process, or store your credit card numbers, debit card details, bank credentials, or physical billing addresses.
  • All purchase transactions are validated anonymously through RevenueCat to issue digital credit top-ups directly to your user wallet.

4. Third-Party Infrastructure Providers

To deliver real-time telecommunication services securely, we integrate with trusted third-party service providers:

  • Firebase (Google LLC): To manage secure anonymous guest sessions, account authentication, and cloud infrastructure.
  • RevenueCat, Inc.: To process, validate, and manage in-app credit purchases and store receipts.
  • Upstream Telecommunication Suppliers: Upstream carrier routers (including TextVerified) strictly to allocate virtual mobile numbers and retrieve carrier SMS payloads. No personal user identifiers are ever shared with these suppliers.
  • Oaknova Systems Infrastructure: Our secure API backend hosted at oaknovasystems.com manages ledger accounting, real-time SSE streaming, and verification lifecycles.

5. Data Retention & Permanent Account Deletion

In full compliance with Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v) and Google Play Data Safety guidelines, you have total control over your data:

  • Temporary Nature: One-time verification phone numbers and received SMS codes expire automatically after their active window (typically 10 minutes) and are not retained indefinitely.
  • Self-Service Account Deletion: You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time directly within the app by going to Settings → Delete Account & Data.
  • Upon account deletion, your user profile, credit wallet, verification history, rental lines, and conversation logs are permanently purged from our PostgreSQL database immediately.

6. Terms of Use (EULA)

By using TempNum, you agree to our terms of service and acknowledge that numbers must not be used for illegal activities, harassment, or financial fraud. For iOS users, our licensing terms conform strictly to the standard Apple End User License Agreement (EULA).

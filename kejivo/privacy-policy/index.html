Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 7, 2026

Welcome to Kejivo ("the Platform," "we," "our," or "us"), a crowdsourced housing opportunity marketplace owned and operated by Oaknova Systems LTD (Registration No. PVT-8Z1K5J63), a company registered under the laws of the Republic of Kenya.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use our website (kejivo.com), our mobile applications on iOS and Android, and our related digital services.

Our Core Lead Privacy Principle

Sensitive housing opportunity information — specifically direct caretaker phone numbers and exact street address hints — is encrypted and hidden from the general public. This sensitive data is only revealed to authenticated users who legitimately unlock an opportunity through the platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, as well as data gathered automatically when interacting with our services:

  • Account & Contact Information: Your name, email address, phone number, and profile photograph. We verify mobile numbers using Firebase Phone Authentication to maintain platform trust and prevent fraud.
  • Contributor Verification Data: If you register as a Contributor to earn rewards, we may collect your national identification number, professional designation (e.g., caretaker, agent, scout), and verified contact details to ensure platform integrity.
  • Housing Listing & Opportunity Data: Property titles, descriptions, rental amounts, bedroom counts, general neighborhood hints, and media (photographs and walkthrough videos) uploaded to showcase vacant houses.
  • Geographic Location Data: With your explicit permission, we collect coarse or precise GPS coordinates to calculate proximity and deliver accurate radius searches for available houses in your area.
  • Communications: In-app chat messages exchanged between seekers and contributors regarding property viewings, inquiries, and dispute submissions.
  • Transaction & Ledger Information: Records of credits purchased, platform facilitation fees paid, and external transaction verification reports. We do not store your credit card numbers, bank account passwords, or M-Pesa PINs.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal information for lawful business and marketplace purposes, including:

  • Facilitating the discovery and unlocking of genuine rental and property opportunities.
  • Verifying contributor credibility and preventing duplicate, outdated, or fraudulent property listings.
  • Sending essential transactional receipts, opportunity unlock details, and viewing confirmations via email and push notifications.
  • Calculating geographic distances for local map and radius searches.
  • Facilitating dispute resolutions and managing user trust ratings.
  • Complying with regulatory obligations under the Kenya Data Protection Act (KDPA), 2019.

3. Payments and Financial Data

Kejivo operates as a technology facilitator. We charge a service fee (via credits) for access to verified lead information. Kejivo does not collect, hold, or escrow rent deposits between seekers and landlords.

  • Web Payments (Paystack): Payments made via M-Pesa, debit/credit cards, or Apple Pay on our website are processed through Paystack Payments Limited, a PCI-DSS certified payment gateway. Your financial credentials are transmitted directly to Paystack and are never stored on our servers.
  • Mobile In-App Purchases: Payments on our iOS and Android mobile apps are processed through the Apple App Store and Google Play Store, managed securely via RevenueCat.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We share data with trusted third-party service providers solely to operate our infrastructure:

  • Firebase Authentication (Google LLC): For secure user sign-in and automated SMS phone number verification.
  • Cloudflare R2: For distributed, encrypted storage of uploaded listing photos and walkthrough videos.
  • Resend: For transactional email delivery (e.g., viewing contact details and purchase receipts).
  • Expo Push Services: For real-time mobile push notifications regarding inquiries and unlocked leads.
  • Paystack & RevenueCat: For payment routing and in-app purchase validation.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your personal information only as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide our services:

  • Listing Deletion: When a listing is removed, associated images and media references are unlinked and permanently deleted.
  • Account Deletion: You have the legal right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal records at any time. You can submit a deletion request directly within the mobile app settings or by contacting support@kejivo.com. Upon receipt, your personal identifiers will be deleted within thirty (30) days, subject to mandatory legal record-keeping requirements.

6. Security of Your Data

We employ modern administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal data. All web and mobile communications are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Cloud storage repositories enforce strict access-control policies to prevent unauthorized data exposure.

7. Rights Under the Kenya Data Protection Act

In accordance with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, users have the right to be informed of how their data is used, access their personal information, object to processing, request correction of false data, and request erasure of outdated or unlawfully held records.

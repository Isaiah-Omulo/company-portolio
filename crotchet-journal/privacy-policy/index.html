Crotchet

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 7, 2026

Welcome to Crotchet: Crochet Project Journal & Tracker ("we," "our," or "us"). We are dedicated to providing a premium tracking companion for your crochet creations, row counts, and yarn inventories. This Privacy Policy details how we handle, store, and sync your personal and project information.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our specialized journaling and cloud-syncing services, we collect and utilize the following information:

  • Account Data: When you register or link an account (using email/password or Google authentication), we process your name and email address to securely maintain and identify your cloud database space.
  • Project & Inventory Data: We store details about your creations (project names, current status, notes, row progress, and session timers) and yarn inventory (colorways, yarn weights, amounts, and brands).
  • Camera & Photo Library Access: We request permission to access your camera and gallery solely for uploading project and material photos to your local gallery and cloud catalog.
  • Local File Storage: We require read and write access to your local filesystem to cache pattern files (such as PDFs or images) for fast, offline viewing.
Cloud Sync & Account Linking

Crotchet features seamless Cloud Syncing. If you begin as a guest (anonymous user), your data is stored locally. When you decide to create a permanent email or Google account, your existing local projects and yarn inventory are fully linked and synced to your permanent profile, preventing any data loss.

2. Data Storage and Retention

We prioritize local performance while offering cloud reliability:

  • Device Cache: Your active project records, inventory lists, and cached pattern files are kept locally using secure persistent memory (AsyncStorage) and local app directories for fast offline access.
  • Firestore Cloud Sync: If you are authenticated, your project and inventory updates are instantly synced to our secure Firebase Firestore cloud database.

3. Subscriptions and Payments

Crotchet offers premium features and subscriptions. All payment verification is processed securely through Apple App Store, Google Play Store, and RevenueCat.

  • We do not collect, view, or store your credit card details or financial information.
  • Subscription states are monitored via securely linked, anonymous store receipts.

4. Third-Party Services

We partner with trusted platforms to ensure app functionality:

  • Firebase (Google): To power authentication services and store database records.
  • RevenueCat: To manage in-app subscription entitlements securely.
  • Google Play Store / Apple App Store: For billing and app distribution.

5. Data Control & Deletion

You have full control over your data. You can delete individual projects, inventory items, or clear your cache anytime inside the app. If you wish to delete your entire cloud account and all associated cloud data, you can do so through the Settings page or by contacting us.
